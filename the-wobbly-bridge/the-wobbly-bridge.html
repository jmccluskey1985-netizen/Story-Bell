The Wobbly Bridge
The Wobbly Bridge
[IMAGETITLE] The Wobbly Bridge
[IMAGE1] Little Finn the fox loved to roam,
Through the forest far from home,
He'd sniff the flowers, chase the bees,
And climb up all the tallest trees.
[IMAGE2] But one fine day he went too far,
Past the stream and past the bar,
He found himself across a gap,
With no clear way to get back.
[IMAGE3] Before him stood a bridge so old,
Its wooden planks were cracked and cold,
It creaked and swayed from side to side,
"Oh my," said Finn, his eyes grew wide.
[IMAGE4] His little legs began to shake,
His tummy did a nervous quake,
"I cannot cross, it's much too scary,
Those wobbly boards look old and airy!"
[IMAGE5] But then he thought of home so warm,
Of Mummy's hugs safe from the storm,
He took a breath, one big and deep,
"I can be brave, I'll take a leap."
[IMAGE6] One tiny paw upon the wood,
He whispered soft, "I know I could,"
The bridge did creak, the bridge did sway,
But Finn kept going anyway.
[IMAGE7] Step by step across he went,
His courage strong, his focus bent,
The wind did blow, the ropes did swing,
But Finn held on to everything.
[IMAGE8] And then at last he reached the end!
He'd crossed the bridge around the bend!
He jumped for joy and gave a cheer,
"I did it! I faced my fear!"
Now Finn knows deep inside his heart,
Being brave is just the start,
You don't need to feel no fright,
Just take one step - you'll be alright.
The End.
